The Problem

Why Traditional Aid Surveys Are Too Slow

Somali families often face sudden economic hardship—from severe droughts that harm crops and livestock to rising food prices. Traditional aid programs rely on slow paper surveys that take months to collect and analyze. By the time help arrives, families have already suffered severe loss.

Wadaag Family Shield Active

Moving from Slow Paperwork to Instant Action

Old Method: Paper Surveys 6 to 12 Months Delayed

Teams travel to remote communities with paper forms. These forms sit in review queues for months, meaning emergency aid is often released long after the dry season has passed.

WADAAG AI: Instant Household Assessment Results in Seconds
